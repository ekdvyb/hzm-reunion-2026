## Practical notes

- **Profile picture (20):** designed inside a circular safe area, so it survives
  round crops on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook.
- **X / Facebook cover (19):** all text sits away from the edges that the
  platforms crop differently on mobile and desktop.
- **YouTube cover (18):** title and details are inside the central safe band
  (the area that is always visible on TV, desktop and mobile).
- **Stories (13–15):** critical text is kept in the middle 80% so the platform
  UI at the top and bottom does not cover it.
- **Print:** `00-cover-8k-enhanced-4320px.png` is 4320 px wide (≈ 36 cm at
  300 dpi). For A2/A1 print, ask for a vector export of the new layouts instead
  of enlarging this file.
- **Quality:** the 21 posts are drawn from scratch at their final resolution,
  so type and edges are genuinely sharp — no upscaling. The original cover is a
  raster image (largest embedded copy 2520 × 3564); the 8K master is a careful
  Lanczos enhancement of that file, not invented detail.

Source script: `_work/generate.py` (rerun with `python3 _work/generate.py`).
