# Harare Zimbabwe Mission calls every comrade home for Reunion 2026

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**
**HARARE, ZIMBABWE — 5 November 2026**

The Harare Zimbabwe Mission will gather its comrades, families and friends for a homecoming on **Saturday 5 December 2026**. The day begins at **The Temple at 10:00 AM** and continues at **Kingfisher Park, Harare**, where every generation of the mission family is invited to share a table.

The reunion is built around a simple idea: nobody should arrive as a stranger. Former missionaries, local members, children and friends are all welcome. A mascot and a jumping castle will keep the youngest guests busy while the stories run long.

For those who are willing, the committee has organised group travel from The Temple to Kingfisher Park, so that the journey itself becomes part of the reunion.

> “Come and be together again,” said committee member Elvis Kwaramba. “That is the whole invitation. The rest is lunch, laughter and photographs.”

The committee has produced a full campaign of invitations, posters, social media assets and a public microsite so that every comrade can find the details and bring someone with them.

## Notes to editors

- **Event:** Harare Zimbabwe Mission Reunion 2026
- **Date:** Saturday 5 December 2026, from 10:00 AM
- **Venue:** The Temple, Harare, then Kingfisher Park, Harare
- **Admission:** Open to all comrades, families and friends. Media welcome; please call ahead for parking and photographs.
