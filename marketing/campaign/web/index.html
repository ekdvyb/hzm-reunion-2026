A homecoming

Every comrade.
Every family.

On Saturday 5 December 2026 the Harare Zimbabwe Mission family gathers again: the people who served, the families who grew up around the mission, and the friends who have heard the stories for years.

We begin at The Temple at 10:00 AM, then travel together to Kingfisher Park — if you are willing. Bring your family, your photographs and your appetite for a long lunch.
